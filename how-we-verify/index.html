Method

How verification works here.

Five rules, applied to every row on the site. They are the reason some cells are empty.

1. A claim needs a document, not a memory

Every fact in a specification table was read on the page named in the source column. Product facts come from the Amazon detail page for that ASIN. Policy facts come from Amazon's Associates pages, the FTC's guidance or the eCFR. If a document could not be opened, the claim is not made.

2. Blank beats plausible

If a value cannot be confirmed, the cell is left empty and marked unverified. Filling a gap with what a similar product usually costs, weighs or measures would be a fabrication, and it is the failure mode this project exists to avoid.

3. Intended and observed are never merged

A short link tells you what someone meant to send. It does not tell you where the link goes, and those two things can disagree. Each submitted link therefore gets two separate sets of fields — intended ASIN, official listing URL, confirmation date and source; and observed redirect target, observed ASIN and observation date. Neither set is ever used to fill the other. S06

4. Policy is quoted, not summarised from memory

Where a rule drives a design decision — no prices, no star ratings, disclosure on every page, links not routed through this domain — the rule is quoted and linked. The source library holds the full list with the date each page states.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not claim to have tested a product. Nothing here is a hands-on measurement.
  • We do not reproduce amazon star ratings or review text. S02
  • We do not display prices or offer price alerts. S02
  • We do not publish traffic, conversion or earnings figures we cannot source to a first-party report. S02
  • We do not route outbound links through this domain. There is no cloaking, no redirect hop, and no JavaScript interstitial. S02

What a validated Special Link would require

Amazon's own definition is mechanical: a Special Link is a link that “properly use[s] the special ‘tagged' link formats we provide”. Validation therefore needs two things that cannot be done from outside an account: confirming the destination resolves to the intended product, and confirming the click appears in the Associates tracking report.
