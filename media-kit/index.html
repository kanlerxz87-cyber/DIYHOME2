About & media kit

A small publication with a short shelf.

DIY Home Upgrades covers home improvements that cost less than a weekend and fit in a cabinet. It publishes rarely and verifies heavily.

What the site is

An independently published product journal. Each entry is a single product, a specification table transcribed from the manufacturer's listing, a plain statement of who it suits, and a plain statement of what could not be verified.

What the site is not

A review site. No product here has been handled, installed or measured by this publication. Calling an entry a review would misrepresent what it is, so they are called entries.

Authorship

Published by the site owner and maintained as an open repository. Every change is a commit, and the repository is public — including the data files behind every table.
