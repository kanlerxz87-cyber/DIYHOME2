Flagged for Review

Flagged for Review

Every irregularity, gap and unverifiable item in this build — published plainly, with source links, so you can review after the site is live and request changes. Flagged items do not block the merge. Sorted by severity.

Nothing on this page is decoration. Each item needs either an official-source check by you, or an explicit keep/change decision. Nothing here is claimed to be fixed.
F-001high

All Amazon product attributes are unverified

This build environment cannot reach amazon.com, link.amazon, or affiliate-program.amazon.com (connections fail). No product title, price, currency, rating, review count, Prime eligibility, availability, dimension or image could be checked against an official source. All such fields are blank or 'unverified' in products.json and are rendered as blank cells in the tables. No values were inferred.

Required action: Owner: open each listing (or Associates Central) and fill products.json / product-intent-confirmation.json, or authorize a rebuild with network access to Amazon.

Sources: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0hmGG4m2

F-003high

Compliance status of the supplied link.amazon short links is unresolved

The brief asked to replace ambiguous outbound links with validated Special Links and to confirm destination and tracking reports before scaling. That validation requires opening the links in Associates Central or a browser; neither was possible here. The short links are therefore NOT claimed to be valid or invalid — their status is unresolved. They are used as-is with rel='sponsored nofollow noopener' and target=_blank. Special Links must properly use the tagged formats provided by the Program (Operating Agreement, 'Special Links' definition).

Required action: Owner: in Associates Central, test one link per product via the enrolled workflow, confirm the destination and the tracking report, then record observed_redirect_targets and confirmation_date/source in product-intent-confirmation.json before scaling traffic.

Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement · https://link.amazon/B0hmGG4m2

F-004high

Intended ASINs are not bound to any product, category or claim

The ASINs B0hmGG4m2, B03QDuGdo and B0hpD2JGQ come from the submitted URLs only. No ASIN could be confirmed to refer to an air fryer, any other kitchen appliance, or any particular product. The air-fryer editorial hypotheses (from the brief) are published as working titles and explicitly NOT as claims about these products.

Required action: Owner: confirm each ASIN's product page and, if these are indeed air fryers, say so explicitly; until then the site makes no product-category claim.

Sources: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0hmGG4m2

F-002medium

Duplicate submitted link: B03QDuGdo appears twice

The project brief lists https://link.amazon/B03QDuGdo two times. The duplicate was deduplicated in the confirmation register (P-002). It may be a copy/paste error in the brief; the owner may have intended to submit a fourth, distinct link.

Required action: Owner: confirm whether a fourth link was intended; if so, submit it and the register entry will be created on rebuild.

Sources: https://link.amazon/B03QDuGdo

F-005medium

Candidate listing URLs are constructed, not verified

https://www.amazon.com/dp/<ASIN> is the standard canonical pattern, recorded in product-intent-confirmation.json as candidate_listing_url with candidate_listing_url_verified=false. It is stored separately from official_listing_url (null) and from observed_redirect_targets (empty). The two field groups must never be merged.

Required action: Owner: open each candidate URL; if it resolves, set official_listing_url, confirmation_date and confirmation_source. Leave candidate fields for audit history.

Sources: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0hmGG4m2 · https://www.amazon.com/dp/B03QDuGdo · https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0hpD2JGQ

F-006medium

Google Search Console verification not performed (SG-29)

Search Console ownership for https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/DIYHOME2/ cannot be completed from this environment (it requires the owner's Google account). No google-site-verification meta tag was fabricated. search-console instructions for the owner are on the Measurement page.

Required action: Owner: add the property in Search Console (URL-prefix, https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/DIYHOME2/), verify via HTML file upload or meta tag, and submit docs/sitemap.xml.

Sources: https://search.google.com/search-console

F-007medium

Reader capture is inert until an endpoint is configured (SG-30)

The reader-capture form on every page and the dashboard store nothing and send nothing unless PUBLIC_ANALYTICS_ENDPOINT is set in docs/assets/js/analytics-config.js. This is deliberate: no email addresses may leave the visitor's browser to an unconfigured destination.

Required action: Owner: deploy the included stdlib collector (tools/collector.py) or wire any privacy-preserving endpoint; then set it in analytics-config.js and rebuild.

Sources: https://github.com/kanlerxz87-cyber/DIYHOME2

F-008medium

Campaign ledger is empty by design; no conversion data exists

data/campaign-ledger.csv contains headers only (recorded_at, source, channel, eligible_sessions, clicks, reported_orders, order_value, provenance). No rows are added without authorized first-party reports (Associates Central). No conversion score or revenue forecast is presented anywhere on the site.

Required action: Owner: after Special Links are confirmed (F-003), populate from Associates Central reports with provenance, then schedule measurement reviews.

Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/

F-009medium

Operating Agreement disclosure placement needs owner review

The sentence 'As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.' appears verbatim under the masthead (above the fold) and in the footer of every generated page, and FTC Endorsement Guides material-connection standards were applied as documented. However, the Agreement is updated from time to time; clause numbering and exact wording were verified only via search excerpts (see Verification page). A lawyer's or the owner's final read-through is advisable before paid traffic.

Required action: Owner: re-read the current Operating Agreement clauses 5 and 7 and the FTC Endorsement Guides; confirm disclosure wording and placement remain compliant.

Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement · https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews

F-010low

No popularity, search-volume or superiority claims anywhere on the site

No keyword research, search-volume or trend data was available in this environment. Guide titles are working hypotheses; the site states this explicitly wherever titles appear. Ranking claims, 'best-selling' language and conversion forecasts have been deliberately omitted.

Required action: Owner: once Search Console and the 5-event analytics collect data (pages 30+ days old), choose the next topics from measured queries; update guides.json and rebuild.

Sources: none external (internal build artifact)
