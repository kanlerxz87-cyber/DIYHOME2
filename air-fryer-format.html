Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Compare the space, cleanup and batch size before choosing a format.

DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · 2026-11-21

Compare usable space, not just capacity

Pick a meal you cook often. Check tray or basket dimensions and how much of that meal fits in one batch. Basket litres and usable area are different numbers.

Make room for use and cleanup

Measure the space needed to open the door or pull the basket out. Check the manual for clearance requirements and which parts are dishwasher safe. The {worksheet} helps with the measuring part.
