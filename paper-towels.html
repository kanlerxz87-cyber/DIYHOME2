Where should an under-cabinet paper towel holder go?

Find a spot that works with a full roll.

DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · 2026-11-20

Try the reach

Hold a full roll where you’d mount the holder. Check whether you can reach it with one hand without bumping cabinet doors or blocking the counter.

Compare mounting options

Check the maker’s surface requirements before choosing adhesive. If you’re considering screws, confirm cabinet thickness and whether you have permission to drill.
