Buyer questions

Six questions that decide whether to trust a link, each answered by the document or listing that settles it. Every Amazon link on this site is a paid link; none of them has earned a confirmed commission.

Are the prices on this site current?

There are no prices on this site. Amazon permits a published price only when Amazon serves the link or the data arrives through Creators API or PA API. Neither is connected here, so the site sends you to the live listing instead of freezing a number that will be wrong by the time you read it.

Basis: S02 · price display

Why are there no star ratings?

Customer star ratings, review counts and review text may be shown only when they were obtained through Creators API or PA API. Copying a rating off the page into a review box is a policy breach, not a shortcut.

Basis: S02 · reviews and ratings

Do these product links pay you?

Yes. Every Amazon link on this site is a paid link and is labelled as one. The submitted tracking ID shopnow047c-20 has not been confirmed as live in the Associates account, so no commission is claimed anywhere on the site, and nothing here should be read as a promise that a purchase will earn one.

Basis: S01 · identifying yourself

Have you used these products?

No. Nobody on this desk has hung the wallpaper, mounted the holder or loaded the rack with pans. The pages compare what the listing publishes, point out what it leaves out or contradicts, and tell you what to measure before you order. That is the whole method, and it is written on the editorial standards page.

Basis: S01 · fair and accurate statements

How do I check that a product is the one you describe?

Every Amazon product has a 10-character identifier in its URL, after /dp/. The entry table on the review page records the identifier each submitted link landed on, with the date it was read, so you can open the same page and compare it yourself.

Basis: S10 · ASIN definition

The pan organizer looks too small for my pans. What should I do?

The listing itself is inconsistent: the title and style say lightweight cookware up to 11 inches, while bullet 4 says up to 12 inches. Measure the widest pan you actually own, including its handle, against the layout you plan to build, and check the newer-model banner Amazon shows at the top of the listing before you commit.
