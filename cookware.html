Will a pan organizer fit your cabinet?

Measure with the pans you already use.

DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · 2026-11-21

Measure the opening

Record usable height, width and depth, including hinges or a face frame that narrows the entry. Measure your widest pan with its handle and your tallest lid.

Pick the layout before the height

The listing publishes three tool-free layouts with different footprints, from 21.31 inches wide to 11.75 inches wide. Choose the layout your cabinet allows, then buy the height that fits above it.

What the listing actually says

  • Attribute table: iron, black, 9.17″D × 21.31″W × 16.61″H.
  • Eight tiers, assembled without tools.
  • Three layouts are published: vertical both sides 21.31 × 9.17 × 16.61 in, vertical one side 11.75 × 9.17 × 16.61 in, horizontal one side 16.61 × 9.17 × 11.75 in.
  • Four colour variants: Black, Bronze, Silver and White. Eight height options are listed.
  • Sold by ORDORA Official and ships from Amazon.
  • Amazon shows a "newer model of this item" banner pointing at ASIN B0BYYTQMTR (20.8 in, 8-tier, stated maximum 120 lb).

What it leaves out

  • The title and item highlight say cookware "up to 11 inches"; bullet 4 on the same page says "up to 12 inches". Neither is corrected here — measure your widest pan against the layout you plan to build.
  • A newer, heavier-rated model exists on the same listing family. Check that banner before ordering.
