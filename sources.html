What these documents constrain

Six rules follow directly from the sources above. They are why this site shows no prices, no star ratings and no price alerts, and why product links are plain rather than shortened.

IDConstraintBasisEffect on this site
C1No prices may be displayed without API access or an Amazon-served link.S02No price appears anywhere. Price comparison cannot be offered.
C2No star ratings, review counts or review text may be displayed without API access.S02No rating appears anywhere. A rating column would be a policy breach, not a design choice.
C3No price tracking or price alerting.S02A price-history or deal-alert feature was excluded deliberately, not overlooked.
C4Filtering or styling a link so it is unclear it goes to Amazon is prohibited.S02Links are plain, unabbreviated Amazon URLs. No cloaking, no intermediary.
C5A purchase arriving through an intermediate site without an affirmative action there is disqualified.S02Click tracking may not route the visitor through this domain. A beacon is the compliant pattern.
C6A short code is not an ASIN and must not be recorded as one.S06, S10, S11, S12Intended and observed fields stay separate; the intended fields remain blank.

Amazon listing and redirector URLs print as plain text so they can be copied and checked by hand. A citation is evidence, not an offer; linking an untagged Amazon page would put a non-Special link on the site. Product pages a reader is invited to visit appear on the shop and the guides, and every one carries a paid-link notice.
