Editorial standards

By the DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk. Buying checklists, not hands-on reviews.

Publication gate

Confirm product intent against an official listing and save the date and source separately from the redirect. Generate a Special Link through enrolled Associates tools, then verify the destination and authorized tracking report before publishing it. Keep a paid-link notice next to each approved link and the Associates notice on every page.

Review the Operating Agreement, Amazon’s Program Policies and FTC endorsement guidance before publishing. Do not scrape Amazon or use product images, ratings, prices or API content without confirming rights, sourcing and freshness requirements. The masthead photograph is AI-generated editorial imagery, not a product depiction.

Publishing rhythm

Proposed: one question-led guide every two weeks, with a monthly evidence and link review. Start with walls and kitchen storage; revisit seasonal topics only when they fit reader needs. Every new entry in the guide register updates the sitemap and RSS feed on build.
