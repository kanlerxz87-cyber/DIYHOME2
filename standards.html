Editorial standards

By the DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk. Buying checklists and listing checks, not hands-on reviews.

Publication gate

Before a product link goes out: confirm the listing identity against the official page, save the date and the source separately from the redirect observation, then generate a Special Link through enrolled Associates tools and confirm both the destination and the tracking report. Keep the words "paid link" beside the link and the Associates statement on every page. Until those steps happen, the site links with the submitted tag and says plainly that no commission has been confirmed.

Review the Operating Agreement ↗, the Program Policies ↗ and the FTC endorsement guidance ↗ before publishing. Every document behind every claim, with the date it states and the constraint it imposes, sits in the citation register. Do not scrape Amazon, and do not reproduce its product images, ratings, prices or API content without confirming rights, sourcing and freshness. The photography on this site is AI-generated editorial imagery, not a product depiction.

What this desk will not write

  • A price, because it will be wrong by the time anyone reads it, and because publishing one without API-served data is prohibited.
  • A star rating or a review count, for the same reason.
  • A specification the listing does not publish. Where a listing contradicts itself, both values are quoted and the contradiction is flagged — see the pans organizer.
  • A hands-on claim. Nobody here has loaded the rack with pans or hung the wallpaper.
  • A traffic or revenue claim. The ledger is empty and says so.

Publishing rhythm

One question-led guide per fortnight, with a monthly evidence and link review. Topic choice should be driven by measured queries once a collector and Search Console are live; until then it is editorial judgement, labelled as such. Every new guide updates the sitemap and the RSS feed on build, and the build fails if the two disagree.

Corrections

Corrections are dated and listed on the Flagged for Review page, including the 6 made in this pass. A corrected page keeps its original date and gains a note; it does not silently change.
