Methodology

  1. Sources allowed: Associates Central reports (clicks, orders, shipped revenue), Search Console (queries, impressions, clicks) — nothing else without the owner's written instruction.
  2. Eligible sessions: sessions on pages where at least one sponsored link appears, per the analytics endpoint, once configured. Until then this field stays empty.
  3. Provenance is mandatory: every row records which report, date range, and download it came from. No row, no claim.
  4. Timestamped: recorded_at is the moment the row was entered, not the order date.
  5. No modeling: this ledger never contains modeled, estimated or forecast values. Absence of data is reported as absence.

Priority ladder from the brief: measurement before automation. This ledger is the instrument; the five-event analytics in the measurement plan is the signal; automation comes after both prove out.
