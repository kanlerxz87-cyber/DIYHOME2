Privacy & measurement

What this site stores

  • localStorage["dh_events_v1"] — an array of anonymous event objects (page_view, guide_read, affiliate_outbound_click, feed_subscribe_click, reader_capture_submit) with timestamp, path and — for clicks — the destination host only. No cookies. No fingerprinting. No persistent ID.
  • No email addresses are stored by this site unless and until the owner configures the capture endpoint; the capture form is inert otherwise.
  • No advertising, analytics or social-media third-party scripts are loaded. Amazon does not receive any data from this site beyond the click itself when you choose to follow an outbound link.

What this site never stores

  • Names, addresses, precise location, device fingerprints, cross-site identifiers.
  • Reading history beyond this browser's local event list, which you can erase anytime from the dashboard ("Erase local analytics data") or by clearing site storage.

Your choices

Blocking JavaScript or storage keeps the site fully readable — only the five counters and the capture form degrade. Outbound clicks always reach Amazon with the link as published; Amazon's own privacy practices apply on their site: amazon.com/privacy.
