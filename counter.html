Will an under-cabinet towel holder fit?

DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · November 20, 2026

Measure a full roll, not just the cardboard core. Mock up the holder with tape and check whether the roll can slide on without hitting a wall. Compare adhesive and screw mounting against the cabinet maker’s instructions; check cabinet thickness before drilling.
