Provenance rules

  • No conversion score is calculated or displayed from this ledger.
  • No revenue forecast is derived from this ledger.
  • No row may be back-filled from memory, from a search engine result, or from a third-party estimator.
  • Rows are never deleted. Corrections are appended as new rows referencing the row they correct.
  • Our own outbound_click counts and Amazon's reported clicks are different measurements and are never added together.

Why this is the right way round

It is easy to publish a dashboard that looks like it is working: a rising line, a conversion percentage, an earnings projection. Every one of those numbers would be invented, because the underlying reports sit behind an Associates sign-in. An empty table with a strict schema is less exciting and it is true.

Where the numbers will come from

Two authorised Amazon reports and the site's own counter, kept apart on purpose:

  • Associates click report — supplies clicks, per link and per day.
  • Associates earnings report — supplies reported_orders.
  • This site's counter — supplies outbound intent only. It is recorded in a different place and never summed with Amazon's numbers.
