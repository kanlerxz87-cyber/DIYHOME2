What the evidence does not support

Four things, and each one is a decision the owner has to make rather than a task an agent can finish:

  • Intended product identity. A redirect observation is not a statement of intent. Until the intended ASIN is written down inside the enrolled workflow, the intended fields stay empty.
  • Special Link compliance. The submitted short codes are 9 characters and resolve to 10-character ASINs. Whether the supplied links are compliant is unresolved — S02 ↗ requires the tag in the URL of each link placed on a site, and no tag was observable in the redirect from outside the account. The links are not declared invalid.
  • Commission rates per SKU. The public rate card (S04 ↗) publishes category rates, not per-ASIN assignment. The assigned rate and any order data live behind Associates sign-in.
  • Traffic and revenue. No analytics endpoint is deployed on this host, Search Console ownership is not verified, and the campaign ledger is empty by design — not zero.

Next three decisions

  1. Confirm identity, then tag. In Associates Central, generate one link per product with shopnow047c-20, confirm the destination matches the intended product, and check that the click appears in the tracking report. One product is enough to start; do not scale until one works.
  2. Choose the next guide from measured queries. That needs Search Console ownership (verification guide ↗) and a collector. Until then, topic choice is editorial judgement, and this page will not dress it up as data.
  3. Fill the ledger from a first-party report. One exported row is worth more than any forecast. Until it arrives the ledger stays empty, and no conversion rate is calculated.

Enrolment threshold, in plain terms

S09 ↗ describes the application review: at least three qualified sales in the first 180 days, personal orders excluded, and robust original content — a rule of thumb of at least 10 posts. This site publishes eleven pages plus a guide, which meets the content rule of thumb; the sales qualification is not something a build can do. That is the single biggest open item, and it belongs to the account holder.
