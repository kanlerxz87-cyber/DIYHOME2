Executive summary

A buyer-first publication: a shop, four question-shaped guides, and the evidence register kept beside them.

What this site is

Three products across three Amazon categories are published as a scannable shop: the shop index, one page per category, and one table row per product. Every figure in a row comes from the verified record in data/products.json; a field that could not be confirmed is printed as unverified instead of being filled in. The four published guides answer one buyer question each, and no page links to an untagged Amazon product page.
