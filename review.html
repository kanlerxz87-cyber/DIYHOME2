F01Short codes do not match the ASINs they resolve toProduct links · all threehighThree unique short links were supplied. Each resolves to a real Amazon listing, but no short code equals the ASIN it lands on, and all three codes are 9 characters where Amazon defines an ASIN as 10. Example: link.amazon/B0hmGG4m2 resolves to /dp/B08FKW9JRM.
