4. What is not published, and why

No price, no star rating, no review count, no revenue figure and no conversion rate appears anywhere on this site. That is a policy consequence rather than an omission: S02 ↗ permits a price only when Amazon serves the link or the data arrives through Creators API or PA API, permits customer reviews and star ratings only through those APIs, and prohibits price tracking and alerting outright. The 2026 update adds an original-content definition and a 180-day shipping window (S03 change log ↗).
