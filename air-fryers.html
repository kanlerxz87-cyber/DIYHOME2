Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Compare the space, cleanup and batch size before choosing a format.

DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · 2026-11-20

Compare usable space, not just capacity

Pick a meal you cook often. Check the tray or basket dimensions and compare how much of that meal fits in a single batch. For a multi-function appliance, check which accessories are needed for the modes you expect to use.

Make room for use and cleanup

Measure the space needed to open the door or pull the basket out. Check the manual for clearance requirements and which parts can go in the dishwasher.
