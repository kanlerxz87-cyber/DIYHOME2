Why some source URLs are printed instead of linked

Policy, guidance and standards documents are ordinary hyperlinks — they are not product pages and involve no affiliate tag. Amazon product pages and the link.amazon redirectors are printed as plain text so they can be copied into a browser for manual review.

That choice is not cosmetic. Linking an untagged Amazon product URL would place an Amazon link on the site that is not a Special Link, and the Participation Requirements require the Associates ID to appear in the URL of every link placed on the site. Printing the URL keeps the evidence intact while keeping the site clear of that rule. Product pages that the reader is invited to visit appear elsewhere, and every one of those is tagged rel="sponsored". S02
