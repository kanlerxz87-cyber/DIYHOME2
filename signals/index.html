Measurement

Five events, no cookies.

The site measures five things and nothing else. There is no third-party tag, no cookie banner because there are no cookies, and no user identifier. The endpoint ships empty: it is yours to self-host.

The five defined events. Field names are the exact keys sent in the payload.
EventFires whenFieldsWhy
page_view Once per page load, after the disclosure strip has rendered. page_path page_type referrer_host Baseline reach. No user identifier is created.
product_view When a product page reaches the specification table. page_path asin category Distinguishes catalogue interest from general reading.
outbound_click On activation of any link carrying rel="sponsored". page_path asin destination_host link_position The only event that maps to commission intent. Sent as a one-way beacon; the browser navigates straight to Amazon and never through our domain.
guide_complete When a guide page is scrolled to 90% and has been open for 20 seconds or more. page_path guide_slug seconds_bucket Tells us which question was actually answered, not just opened.
subscribe On successful submission of the reader-capture form. page_path capture_status Reader capture. Separate from the advertising funnel.

What the counter does and does not do

The outbound counter sends a single POST to the endpoint with a JSON body, then lets your browser navigate straight to Amazon. The click is never routed through this domain, which matters twice over: cloaking a Special Link is prohibited, and a redirect hop on this domain could be read as an intermediate site. S02

Why a beacon rather than an image pixel: the payload survives navigation, and the browser has no opportunity to cancel it. The request is sent with keepalive, credentials omitted, and no referrer beyond the origin.

Honest status

No endpoint is configured

The analytics script ships with an empty data-endpoint. Until you point it at a collector you host, the five events are emitted to the console and discarded. The site therefore reports no measurement today, and claims none.

What this measurement cannot tell you

  • It cannot tell you whether a click became a sale. Only Amazon's Associates reports can, and those require account access.
  • Its click count is a different measurement from Amazon's reported clicks and must never be added to it.
  • It cannot support a conversion rate, an earnings estimate or a revenue forecast. The campaign ledger explains how reported figures are to be recorded when they arrive.

Respecting the visitor

  • No cookies, no localStorage, no fingerprinting, no cross-site identifiers.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured; when either is set, nothing is sent.
  • IP addresses are not stored by the reference collector.
  • No data is shared with, or sold to, anyone.
