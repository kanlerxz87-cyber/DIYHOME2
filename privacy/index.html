Privacy

Nothing follows you here.

No cookies, no third-party tags, no advertising identifiers, no profile. The site is a set of static files.

What this site stores on your device

Nothing. There are no cookies, no localStorage entries, no session identifiers and no fingerprinting scripts. Filtering in the shop happens in memory and is discarded when you leave the page.

What is measured

Five events, described in full on the measurement page. When an endpoint is configured, a beacon carries the page path, the product identifier and, for outbound clicks, the destination host. It carries no IP address, no cookie and no identifier that could link two visits. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured and suppress everything.

Amazon's own collection

Following a product link takes you to Amazon.com, which is governed by Amazon's privacy notice, not ours. Amazon sets its own cookies and records the referral. Their privacy notice is at amazon.com.

Amazon's Participation Requirements ask associates to disclose the use of cookies by third parties on their site. S02 This site sets none of its own; the third party is Amazon, and it acts after you leave.

Email

There is no email capture running on this site. A subscribe form exists as a placeholder and is not connected to a list; it says so on the page. If that changes, this page changes first.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected by this site, there is nothing to export or erase. If you believe otherwise, or you are a controller with a question, the correction route is the Flagged for review page.
