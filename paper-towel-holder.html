Where should an under-cabinet paper towel holder go?

Find a spot that works with a full roll.

DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · 2026-11-21

Try the reach

Hold a full roll where you would mount the holder. Check that you can reach it one-handed without bumping cabinet doors or blocking the counter.

Compare mounting options

The listing offers adhesive or screws. Adhesive removes cleanly but the listing publishes no load rating, so check the surface and the weight you plan to hang before you trust it.

What it leaves out
