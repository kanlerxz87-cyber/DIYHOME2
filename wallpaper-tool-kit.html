What tools do you need for peel-and-stick wallpaper?

Start with the wall, not the tool kit.

DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · 2026-11-21

Check the surface first

Read the wallpaper maker’s instructions for surface prep and paint cure time. Try a sample in an inconspicuous area before committing to a whole wall.

Plan the tricky cuts

Sketch where seams, corners and trim land. Before buying a bundle, check whether you own a straightedge, a suitable cutting tool and something the wallpaper maker recommends for smoothing.
