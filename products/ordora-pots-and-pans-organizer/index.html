Who it suits

  • Cabinets where pans are stacked and the bottom one is always the wrong one
  • Renters who want a rack that lifts out without leaving marks
  • Anyone whose tallest pan is under the height they choose

What to think about first

  • The listing describes a fit for lightweight cookware up to 11 inches. Heavy cast iron is outside the stated use.
  • Measure the internal height of the cabinet, then pick from the eight listed heights. The tallest option is not automatically the right one.
  • Eight tiers need width as well as height. Mock it up with books before ordering.
