Who it suits

  • Rentals, where an adhesive mount is easier to reverse than four screw holes
  • Kitchens where the roll currently lives on the counter
  • Mounting vertically on a cabinet side when the underside is already crowded

What to think about first

  • Adhesive holds differently on painted MDF, laminate and bare wood. Test on a hidden patch first.
  • Check the clearance under the cabinet before ordering — a full roll needs room to turn.
  • The listing also describes towel-rail, foil-holder and utensil-holder uses. Treat those as the seller’s suggestion, not a tested claim.
