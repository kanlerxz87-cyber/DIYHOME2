What to Check Before Buying a Smoothing Kit

  • Surface Safety: Hard plastic squeegees can scuff matte or textured vinyl if pressed at steep angles; pair firm passes with light pressure or a felt edge.
  • Blade Replacement Access: Vinyl adhesive gums up blades quickly—verify replacement blades are included and standard-sized.
