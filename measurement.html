These are the five events the shipped client actually records. An earlier draft published a different five (product_view, outbound_click, guide_complete, subscribe) that was never implemented; that mismatch is flagged for review rather than silently renamed.

Import only aggregate figures from authorised reports and keep a dated provenance trail. Associates Central ↗ is the only source for reported orders; an outbound click on this site is not a conversion and the two are never added together.

Production telemetry is not connected. GitHub Pages cannot execute the optional collector, and the client ships with no endpoint, so today it logs nothing and the site claims nothing. Reader capture is RSS, without collecting an email address. Search Console ownership is not verified; the sitemap is ready for submission when it is (verification guide ↗).

Full detail, including the privacy behaviour and how to run the collector on another host, sits on the campaign desk and in the repository README.

Why the counters are not shown as results

A page view is not a session, a click is not an order and an opted-in browser is not a visitor. The site publishes no conversion rate because the numerator would be a click it counted itself and the denominator would be a report it does not have. When both exist, they will be published with their provenance — not before.
