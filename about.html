Authorship, Transparency & Methodology

How DIY Home Upgrades Journal Researches & Verifies Products

Readers deserve to know who wrote a guide, where every dimension came from, and how affiliate commissions work—without hunting through fine print.

Editorial Desk & Authorship

All buying guides on this site are authored and maintained by the DIYHOME2 Editorial Desk using a measurement-first editorial standard: we lead with homeowner and renter trade-offs—cabinet clearances, mounting surfaces, and load ceilings—before recommending hardware.

We do not invent hands-on test results we have not performed, we do not copy merchant descriptions verbatim, and we explicitly flag internal listing contradictions whenever official product pages disagree with themselves.

Affiliate Disclosure & Independence
