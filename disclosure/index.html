Disclosure

How this site makes money.

Plainly: through the Amazon Associates programme. You pay the same price whether you use our links or not, and no brand has paid for a mention on this site.

The required statement

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Quoted verbatim from §5 of the Associates Program Operating Agreement, which requires it to appear clearly and prominently on the site. S01

What that means in practice

  • When you follow a product link and buy something, Amazon pays this site a commission. The commission comes out of Amazon's margin, not out of your price.
  • Every outbound product link is marked rel="sponsored" and announces itself as sponsored in the surrounding text.
  • No retailer, brand or agency has paid for coverage, ranking or a favourable sentence. There is no sponsored content on this site.
  • Affiliate status never buys a product a place on the register. Being on the register means we could verify the listing.

Where the statement appears

On every page, twice: in the strip directly beneath the masthead, and again in the footer. This is a deliberate reading of the FTC's placement guidance, which says a disclosure should be where it “easily catches consumers' attention and is difficult to miss”, and warns that a disclosure at the bottom of a page may be missed. A strip under the masthead is early enough to be read before the content; the footer repeat is redundancy, not a substitute. S04

The operative rule sits at 16 CFR §255.5, which requires disclosure of material connections between an endorser and the seller. S05

What we do not do

  • We do not cloak, shorten or reroute our outbound links. They point directly at Amazon. S02
  • We do not ask you to bookmark a Special Link. S02
  • We do not offer rewards, rebates or incentives for clicking. S02
  • We do not buy search ads against Amazon trademarks. S02
  • We do not publish prices, price alerts or star ratings. S02
  • We do not claim to have tested anything we have not tested.
