How do you smooth peel-and-stick wallpaper?

DIY Home Upgrades editorial desk · November 20, 2026

Before you commit to a whole wall, use an offcut to check how the paper handles. Compare the edge of the smoothing tool with the finish of your wallpaper. Check the wallpaper maker’s instructions for which tools and surfaces are compatible.
